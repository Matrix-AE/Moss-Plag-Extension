## Scope

This Privacy Policy describes how Matrix-AE processes personal and sensitive data for the personal similarity-workflow product. It is published before checkout and must stay truthful to the implemented data map.

## What We Process

Account identifiers, payment metadata (via processor), temporary source code for comparison, BYO provider userid, bearer result URLs, session/device records, IP/diagnostics, and support communications.

## Purpose and Basis

Provide the purchased workflow (contract), obtain submission consent where required, prevent abuse, meet tax/legal obligations, and operate security monitoring.

## Recipients, Transfers, Subprocessors

Payment processor (Stripe), hosting infrastructure, and the similarity provider path. Cross-border transfers use appropriate safeguards. The machine-readable subprocessors list is maintained with change notice.

## Retention and Safeguards

Source retention is temporary (hours-scale backstop). Result bearer URLs remain until you forget/delete them or history purges — **this does not guarantee provider deletion**. Product paths use TLS; secrets and result metadata use encryption at rest.

## Choices and Rights

Forget saved links, delete terminal history, export account metadata (no source bytes), and request account deletion subject to payment/tax legal-retention exceptions.

## Minors

Adults **18+** only. Institutional and minor datasets are outside the personal MVP.

## Claims We Do Not Make

We do **not** claim local-only processing, private reports, guaranteed provider deletion, or end-to-end encryption unless runtime and agreements prove otherwise.
